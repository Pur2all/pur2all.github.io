If you see me around and you are not in the mood for discussing research I'd be happy to chat about any of the following topics!

## Music 
I really spend a lot of time listening to music, mostly music that people labels as noise, e.g., extreme subgenres of metal and punk.  
I also play electric guitar, but only fast stuff. I have been dreaming of having a band for years, but my life as a researcher prevents this from happening.  
As a consequence of all of this, I go to a lot of concerts and support the underground scene.


You can find my:  
- Last.fm profile [here](https://www.last.fm/user/Pur2all).  
- RYM profile [here](https://rateyourmusic.com/~Pur2all).  
- (always not updated) Discogs profile [here](https://www.discogs.com/it/user/pur2all).

## Films
In addition to music, my other great passion is films. I especially like to explore Italian and Asian films, without any particular genre preferences.  
Just to give a taste of my tastes, some of my favorites are "Per qualche dollaro in più", "The Big Lebowski", "Tremors" and "Visitor Q".

## DIY ethic
Being a metalhead/punk comes with stick to the DIY (Do It Yourself) ethic. I really like to craft by myself a lot of stuff, mainly t-shirts, patches and clothing in general.  
I plan to start a graphic design project too.

## Minor hobbies
Even though I spend less time on it than on the things mentioned above, I also like to play videogames, to cook (especially dishes from 
[my culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neapolitan_cuisine)) and last but not least I'm passionate about history and linguistics.
